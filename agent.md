Overall

This design is suitable for a real-world maintenance management system serving around 50 hypermarkets. It supports:

Multi-store operation with store-level access control.
Role-based access (store managers, office staff, workers, administrators).
Maintenance categories with configurable default priorities and default worker assignments.
Multiple worker assignments per ticket.
Planned hours versus actual work logs.
Historical employee rates without affecting past labour calculations.
Multiple expense types with receipts.
Flexible media categories for before, during, and after repair documentation.
Approval, rejection, reconciliation, and ticket closure workflows.
Notifications for all major workflow events.
A complete audit trail through ticket history.
